Topic : StatefulSet + volumeClaimTemplates
-------------------------------------------

(*) What problem does volumeClaimTemplates solve?

 - Suppose we have a database with 3 Pods:
        database-0
        database-1
        database-2

- Each Pod needs its own persistent storage.

- We don't want:
        database-0 ──┐
        database-1 ──┼── same PVC ❌
        database-2 ──┘

- Instead, we want: Each Pod gets its own PVC. That's exactly what volumeClaimTemplates provides.
        database-0 → PVC → PV → Storage
        database-1 → PVC → PV → Storage
        database-2 → PVC → PV → Storage

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(*) What is volumeClaimTemplates?

- volumeClaimTemplates is a field in a StatefulSet that tells Kubernetes: "For every Pod created by this StatefulSet, automatically create a PVC for that Pod."

- Example:
    volumeClaimTemplates:
      - metadata:
          name: data
        spec:
          accessModes:
            - ReadWriteOnce
          resources:
            requests:
              storage: 1Gi

- If the StatefulSet has: replicas: 3... Kubernetes creates:
              Pod: database-0
                 ↓
              PVC: data-database-0
              
              Pod: database-1
                 ↓
              PVC: data-database-1
              
              Pod: database-2
                 ↓
              PVC: data-database-2
And each PVC gets its own PV.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

(*) Complete flow

                                      StatefulSet
                                          │
                                          │ replicas: 3
                                          ↓
                      ┌────────────┬────────────┬────────────┐
                      │ database-0 │ database-1 │ database-2 │
                      └─────┬──────┴─────┬──────┴─────┬──────┘
                            ↓             ↓            ↓
                         PVC-0          PVC-1        PVC-2
                            ↓             ↓            ↓
                           PV-0          PV-1         PV-2
                            ↓             ↓            ↓
                       Storage-0       Storage-1    Storage-2

Why StatefulSet?
  - Stable Pod identity
  - Stable network identity
  - Stable persistent storage

Now volumeClaimTemplates gives us the third:
    StatefulSet
    ├── Stable Pod identity
    ├── Stable network identity
    └── Stable storage identity

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The important behavior

- If:
        database-0
         ↓
      data-database-0
and database-0 gets deleted: database-0 ❌

- Kubernetes can recreate: 
          database-0 ✅
             ↓
          data-database-0

- The PVC remains, so the Pod can get its same persistent data back. That's one of the major reasons StatefulSets are used for databases and other stateful 
applications.
