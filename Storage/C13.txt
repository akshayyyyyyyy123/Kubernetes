* Reclaim Policy

- A Reclaim Policy defines what Kubernetes should do with the underlying storage when a PVC is deleted and the PV is released.
- There are two important policies:

1. Retain
  - The storage/data is not automatically deleted.
  - Useful when you want to protect important data.

                    PVC deleted
                         ↓
                  PV becomes Released
                         ↓
                Underlying data remains


2. Delete
  - The storage is automatically cleaned up.
  - This is commonly used with dynamically provisioned storage.

                    PVC deleted
                       ↓
                    PV deleted
                       ↓
        Underlying dynamically provisioned storage is deleted

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* See our Minikube StorageClasses

# kubectl get sc
NAME                 PROVISIONER                RECLAIMPOLICY   VOLUMEBINDINGMODE   ALLOWVOLUMEEXPANSION   AGE
standard (default)   k8s.io/minikube-hostpath   Delete          Immediate           false                  6d22h


- So our dynamically provisioned PVC:
            dynamic-pvc
                 ↓
          StorageClass: standard
                 ↓
          ReclaimPolicy: Delete

- That's why the dynamically created PV had: 
RECLAIM POLICY
Delete

In some of our prev hands-on:

# kubectl get pv
NAME                                       CAPACITY   ACCESS MODES   RECLAIM POLICY   STATUS     CLAIM                 STORAGECLASS   VOLUMEATTRIBUTESCLASS            AGE
my-pv                                      1Gi        RWO            Retain           Released   default/my-pvc        manual         <unset>                          3d14h
pv-02                                      1Gi        RWO            Retain           Bound      default/pvc-02        manual         <unset>                          24h
pvc-9765cc85-b95f-4fc3-b942-252e95f7e1ec   1Gi        RWO            Delete           Bound      default/dynamic-pvc   standard       <unset>                          21h

- In the above output, my-pv and pv-02 are the volumes created as part of static provisioning, whereas the volume which uses dynamic provisioning "pvc-9765cc85-b95f-4fc3-b942-252e95f7e1ec"
has reclaim policy set to "Delete".

- Our manually created PV had:
    persistentVolumeReclaimPolicy: Retain

- So:
          Manual PV
              ↓
           Retain
          
          Dynamic PV
              ↓
           Delete

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Important distinction

- Reclaim Policy is associated with the PV.
- StorageClass can define the default reclaim policy for dynamically provisioned PVs.
- Reclaim Policy determines what happens to a PersistentVolume and its underlying storage after the PersistentVolumeClaim using it is deleted. 
The main policies are Retain and Delete.
- Retain → Keep the data
  Delete  → Clean up the storage
