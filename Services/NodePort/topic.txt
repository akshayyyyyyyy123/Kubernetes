KUBE-NODEPORTS
- Responsible for finding the correct Service from the NodePort.

KUBE-SERVICES
- Responsible for Finding the correct backend Pods.

KUBE-SEP
- Responsible for Sending the packet to one specific Pod (using DNAT).



**** How does a NodePort Service work internally? ****
"When a NodePort Service is created, Kubernetes allocates a ClusterIP and a NodePort. kube-proxy watches the Service and EndpointSlice objects and programs iptables rules.
Traffic arriving at <NodeIP>:<NodePort> matches the KUBE-NODEPORTS chain, which forwards it to the corresponding Service chain (KUBE-SVC). The Service chain selects one of
the backend endpoints using load-balancing rules and jumps to the endpoint chain (KUBE-SEP). The endpoint chain performs DNAT, rewriting the destination IP from the
Service's ClusterIP to the selected Pod IP and port. The packet is then delivered directly to that Pod."
