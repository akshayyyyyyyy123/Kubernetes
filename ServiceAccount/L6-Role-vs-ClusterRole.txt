1) Role v/s ClusterRole

* Role
  - It is a namespace-scoped definition.
  - A Role grants permissions within a specific namespace where the Role is defined.
  - Example: Allow monitoring-sa to list Pods only in default.


* ClusterRole
  - It is a Cluster-level definition.
  - It can define permissions for resources across namespaces or cluster-scoped resources such as Nodes.
  - Example: Define permission to list Pods across namespaces when used with a ClusterRoleBinding.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2) How the bindings work

-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
|      Resource                          |             Scope of granted access        |
|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
|      Role + RoleBinding                |              One namespace                 |
| ClusterRole + RoleBinding              |              One namespace                 |
| ClusterRole + ClusterRoleBinding       |              Cluster-wide                  |
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

- Remember: a ClusterRole by itself does not grant anyone permissions. It must be bound to a subject.
