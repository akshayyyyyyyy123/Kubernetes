CronJob hands-on

STEP 01: Create the YAML
# touch cronjob-demo.yaml

apiVersion: batch/v1
kind: CronJob
metadata:
  name: heyy-cronjob

spec:
  schedule: "* * * * *"

  jobTemplate:
    spec:
      template:
        spec:
          containers:
            - name: hello
              image: busybox:1.36
              command:
                - sh
                - -c
                - echo "Hello from CronJob - $(date)"

          restartPolicy: Never

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 02: Apply

# kubectl apply -f cronjob-demo.yaml

# kubectl get cronjobs
NAME            SCHEDULE      TIMEZONE   SUSPEND   ACTIVE   LAST SCHEDULE   AGE
heyy-cronjob    * * * * *     <none>     False     1        3s              82s

# kubectl get jobs
NAME                     STATUS     COMPLETIONS   DURATION   AGE
heyy-cronjob-29848870    Complete   1/1           2s         2m17s
heyy-cronjob-29848871    Complete   1/1           3s         77s
heyy-cronjob-29848872    Complete   1/1           3s         17s


That's the key thing we're proving:

CronJob
   │
   ├── creates Job #1
   │       └── Pod
   │
   ├── creates Job #2
   │       └── Pod
   │
   └── creates Job #3
           └── Pod

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 03: Validation
# kubectl logs heyy-cronjob-29848873-b2cww                                                                                                            
Hello from CronJob - Fri Oct  2 09:13:00 UTC 2026

Job = execute a task
CronJob = repeatedly create Jobs on a schedule

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

What you just demonstrated

          CronJob
             ↓
          schedule: "* * * * *"
             ↓
          Every minute
             ↓
          New Job
             ↓
          New Pod
             ↓
          Command executes
             ↓
          Pod completes
