Hands-on JOB

Job: Run a database-backup-like task once

STEP 01: Create job.yaml

apiVersion: batch/v1
kind: Job
metadata:
  name: backup-job-demo
spec:
  template:
    spec:
      containers:
        - name: backup
          image: busybox:1.36
          command:
            - sh
            - -c
            - |
              echo "Starting backup..."
              sleep 30
              echo "Backup completed successfully"

      restartPolicy: Never

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 02: Apply an validate
